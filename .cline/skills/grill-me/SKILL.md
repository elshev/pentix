## Pentix project notes

- The settled answers belong in an openspec change: the design record for the reason, and the spec delta for the behavior. Propose the change with `/opsx:propose` when the interview ends.
- A term that the interview sharpens goes into `GLOSSARY.md` in the same session. Use the `domain-modeling` skill for that step.
